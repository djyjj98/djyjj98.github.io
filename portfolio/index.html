About Me

Where design and technology meet.

My portfolio brings together web development, user-centered design, photography, illustration, and image editing. I enjoy transforming ideas into clear, polished, and accessible digital experiences.

I am currently completing a Bachelor of Science in Web and Digital Design at the University of Maryland Global Campus. Through my coursework, I have developed skills in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, responsive design, and visual communication.

My professional goal is to work as a front-end web developer or web and digital interface designer. I am especially interested in creating websites that are visually engaging, easy to navigate, and useful across different devices.
